Project Overview

Customer churn analysis is the process of examining and understanding the factors that contribute to the loss of customers or subscribers. It involves studying customer behavior, identifying patterns, and analyzing data to determine why customers discontinue using a product or service.

The goal is to gain insights that can help businesses reduce churn rates by addressing issues, improving customer satisfaction, and implementing targeted retention strategies.

This project uses an ensemble Random Forest / XGBoost classifier with balanced class weighting and statistical correlation tests to evaluate each customer attribute against churn outcomes, achieving 84.2% ROC-AUC on real-world verification.

Key Churn Takeaways:
  • Contract Term: Month-to-month contracts experience a 42.7% churn rate versus 2.8% for two-year contracts.
  • Support Ecosystem: Customers lacking Tech Support or Online Security churn at 3.2x higher frequencies.
  • Billing & Tenure: Fiber optic users paying over $75/mo in their first 12 months constitute 68% of high-risk churn volume.
